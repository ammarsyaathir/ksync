Event Verification Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

The 4-pillar forensic verification methodology applied to all Model 2 community submissions.

Pillar 1: Organizer Business Identity & SSM

Promoters submitting major stadium and arena concerts must provide a valid Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia (SSM) registration number cross-checked against business registries.

Pillar 2: Direct Venue Permit Match

The event hall reservation slot must be authenticated with Malaysian venue operators (Axiata Arena, Stadium Malaysia, Zepp KL).

Pillar 3: Ticketing Whitelist Registry

Outbound ticketing checkout URLs are validated against our Malaysian primary ticketing whitelist. Unverified Telegram or bank transfer DMs are automatically rejected.

Pillar 4: Google Gemini AI Multimodal Vision Auditing

Concert artwork is analyzed via Gemini 4 Argon vision models to detect typography tampering, manipulated dates, or fake sponsor logos.
